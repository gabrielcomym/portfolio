# Portfolio Product Brief

## Purpose

Comym is a public portfolio that communicates design leadership across complex technical products. Each case makes the work legible without disclosing client-sensitive operational detail.

## Audience

Hiring managers, prospective collaborators, and product leaders evaluating design leadership across data-heavy, high-stakes systems.

## Content Model

Case pages use a shared editorial structure: project framing, role and tags, Work introduction, three progressive-disclosure chapters, project description, outcome, related projects, and footer. Approved local exports supply all media. The active order is Kedro, Rivendell, WovenLight, PerformanceAI. Refo remains in a private recovery archive outside the deployable project.

The home occupies a static viewport; wheel gestures anywhere on the page move its internal thumbnail carousel. Contact uses the same public email address throughout the site. Detail panels use Close, 8px label-to-copy spacing, and a `#1E1E1E` backdrop at 95% opacity.

Lab is a separate visual gallery reached from the persistent top navigation. It uses the approved local images in `public/media/lab`. Visitors pan the non-interactive floating image field by dragging or scrolling; individual image context is not inferred from filenames.
Its images form a deliberately oversized, continuously wrapping curved field. A GSAP animation ticker drifts it slowly outside React rendering; drag, wheel, touch, and keyboard input move it in either direction without a terminal position. Reduced-motion preference disables ambient drift.

## Rivendell

Rivendell is a public, sanitized case page for a prescriptive-analytics engagement in a mineral-export port operation. It presents how fragmented operational data, predictive models, physical constraints, and optimization logic were translated into a human-in-the-loop planning sandbox for shift-based decisions. Detailed operational artifacts, client context, financial metrics, and impact measurement remain reserved for private case review.

## Experience Principles

- Keep complex systems understandable through an editorial sequence and progressive disclosure.
- Separate reconstructed product logic from confirmed evidence and public claims.
- Preserve user control and explainability when presenting AI, predictive, and optimization workflows.
- Maintain keyboard-accessible, responsive, reduced-motion-aware interactions.
